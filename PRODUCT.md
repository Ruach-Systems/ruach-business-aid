# Business Aid by RUACH

## Platform

Web: .NET 10 Blazor WebAssembly PWA with an ASP.NET Core API, Dapper and SQL Server.

## Product purpose

Business Aid records sales, stock and expenses for business workspaces. It supports
offline operations through an IndexedDB workspace and durable synchronization outbox.
Existing accounts, memberships, business records and audit data must be preserved.

## Approved rebrand boundaries

The public name is **Business Aid by RUACH**. RUACH is the parent identity;
its read-only brand guide is the visual authority. The product needs a distinct
emblem, not the Mashal shield, Living Breath, Royal Exchange or
Royal Folio. C / Balanced Record is agent-selected with owner review pending.
The coordinator authorized reversible non-deploying integration; this does not
establish owner artwork approval. Alternatives remain available.

Preserve workflows, business rules, screen structure, responsive layouts and
interactions. Do not introduce dark-mode functionality or a different backend.
Keep internal solution/assembly/configuration names, cookies, Data Protection
identity and key material, migration identities, browser storage keys, model
version and wire/outbox formats compatible.

## Deployment boundaries

Prepare `https://businessaid.ruachsystems.dev` and
`https://api.businessaid.ruachsystems.dev` without disrupting the existing
`https://businessaid.mashalsystems.com` and
`https://api-businessaid.mashalsystems.com` pair.

No deployment workflow may be triggered. No direct deployment, production
DNS/hosting/database change or cutover is authorized. Provider readiness,
secure configuration and a new explicit user instruction are still required.
Old-origin pending work must remain recoverable; no browser-storage clearing
or automatic cross-origin outbox migration is permitted.
